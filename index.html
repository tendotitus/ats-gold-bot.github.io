How the signal engine works

The app samples independent XAU/USD spot observations approximately every five minutes. It groups those observations into indicative M5, M15 and H1 bars, then checks EMA 20/50, RSI 14 and ATR 14. A signal is shown only when all three timeframes align. This is a transparent rule-based indicator, not machine-learning prediction.

The independent spot feed is indicative mid-market data, not your broker's executable bid/ask price. Prices and levels may differ from XNance. Signals are not guaranteed and this app does not place trades.

Data source: XAUS Gold Data API. Spot feed is cached and sampled; it is not a tick-by-tick trading feed.
